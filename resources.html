Know Your Rights

Why knowing your housing rights matters in South Africa:

In South Africa, where housing is still deeply shaped by the legacy of apartheid and ongoing inequality, knowing your rights is a powerful tool for protection and resistance. Many communities face threats of eviction, poor living conditions, and unfair treatment by landlords or government authorities, often without clear information or support. Understanding your legal rights can help you challenge unlawful evictions, demand dignified housing, and hold those in power accountable. Knowledge is not just empowerment, it's a defense against displacement and a step toward justice.
