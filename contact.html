Contact

We welcome engagement from community members, organizations, researchers, and media interested in housing and human rights. Whether you would like to learn more about our work, share resources, or explore ways to collaborate, we encourage you to reach out.

While we do not provide direct housing assistance, we strive to raise awareness, share information, and connect with others working towards improved living conditions. If you are experiencing housing challenges, we may be able to direct you to relevant resources or advocacy efforts.

For organizations and businesses, we are open to discussions on potential partnerships that align with our mission. Journalists and researchers looking for insights on housing issues in South Africa are also welcome to contact us.

We believe in the power of collective action to highlight housing challenges and work towards meaningful change. Get in touch to learn more or get involved.
